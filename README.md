Chatty Analytics provides a WhatsApp CRM platform that tracks ad campaigns, automates conversations, and offers detailed sales analytics. The service includes AI-powered agents, integration with Meta Conversion API, and customizable plans for businesses of various sizes. It targets companies selling via WhatsApp, offering tools for lead tracking, sales reporting, and multi-channel marketing optimization.

**Areas:** CRM · WhatsApp · Marketing · AI · Analytics · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Chatty Analytics: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
